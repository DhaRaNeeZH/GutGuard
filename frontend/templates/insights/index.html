About These Figures

All charts and percentages on this page are calculated from meals you have logged in GutGuard. Consistency % = days with ≥1 logged meal ÷ days elapsed in the week (not the full 7-day week). An unlogged day does not necessarily mean you skipped meals. No medical thresholds are applied. These are personal habit observations only.
